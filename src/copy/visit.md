## Seating and groups

We don't take reservations, so every table is first come, first served. Good for groups and kids.

## Ordering ahead and delivery

[Order ahead online](https://nonnadelias.direct-ordering.com/order-now) and pick up at the restaurant. We deliver. [Order online](https://nonnadelias.direct-ordering.com/order-now) or call (718) 445-6662.

## Amenities and payment

You'll find outdoor seating, a wheelchair-accessible entrance and free parking. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.

## About us

Nonna Delia's is a pizza restaurant at 18-32 College Point Blvd in Queens, in Flushing, NY. Look for us in College Point, beside Dunkin' and College Point Optometric Associates, near Fisherman Depot and City Fresh Market. [Read our story](/about/).

## Where to find us

Nonna Delia's is at 18-32 College Point Blvd, Queens, NY. We're open Mon–Thu 10 AM to 9:30 PM; Fri–Sat 10 AM to 10:30 PM; Sun 10 AM to 9:30 PM.
