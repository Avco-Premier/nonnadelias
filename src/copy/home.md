Nonna Delia's is a pizza restaurant in Flushing, NY. Come in for lunch and dinner seven days a week.

## What is Nonna Delia's known for?

The menu does the talking: 11 sections and 95 dishes. It runs from pizza and appetizers to soups and salads. If it's your first visit, start with the Upside Down Sicilian Pizza, the Cauliflower Gluten Free Pizza or the Sicilian Cheese Pizza. You can eat in, take out or order delivery. You can order ahead online and pick it up at the restaurant. Prices for every dish are on our menu page.
