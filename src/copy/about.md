Nonna Delia's is a pizza restaurant at 18-32 College Point Blvd in Queens, in Flushing, NY. Look for us in College Point, beside Dunkin' and College Point Optometric Associates, near Fisherman Depot and City Fresh Market. We're open for lunch and dinner, seven days a week.

The heart of the menu is the gourmet pizza section: 12 choices, from the Nonna Famous Grandma Sicilian Pizza to the Nonna Famous Sicilian Alla Vodka Pizza. There's also a pizza list with seven choices. For guests who avoid gluten, the menu has the Mini Gluten Free Pizza and the Cauliflower Gluten Free Pizza.

Guests rate Nonna Delia's 4.2 stars across 464+ Google reviews. “The food was delicious, the staff was incredibly friendly, and the vibes were fantastic!” (Madison DeSoto, Google review) “I was visiting from Chicago and I wanted a great pizza 🍕and omggg can I say what a great amazing pizza!” (Irene Minard, Google review).

You can eat in, take out or order delivery. You can order ahead online and pick it up at the restaurant. There's free street parking and paid street parking. Stop by 18-32 College Point Blvd, or call (718) 445-6662 and we'll have your order ready.

## Before you visit

You'll find outdoor seating, a wheelchair-accessible entrance and free parking. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.
